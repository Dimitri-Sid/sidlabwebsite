Why do some patients respond to immunotherapy while others do not? Why do some patients control their disease better than others? We profile tumors and blood longitudinally from patients using cutting-edge technologies to map how immune cells, tertiary lymphoid structures, and the tumor microenvironment change with treatment, shedding light on the biological features that shape patient outcomes.
