Can cancer be stopped before it becomes invasive? We study the immune changes that accompany early carcinogenesis, from precursor lesions to high-risk populations, to identify emerging tumor antigens and immune signatures, develop approaches for assessing cancer risk, and test vaccine-based and other strategies for cancer interception.
