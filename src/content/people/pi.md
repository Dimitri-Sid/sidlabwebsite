Dr. Sidiropoulos is an Assistant Professor of Oncology at the Johns Hopkins University School of Medicine, with appointments in the Divisions of Cancer Immunotherapy, Gastrointestinal Oncology, and Quantitative Sciences. His research program integrates translational immunology, data science and AI to define tumor-immune dynamics and develop new strategies for cancer immunotherapy and interception. He received his PhD in Cellular and Molecular Medicine from the Johns Hopkins University School of Medicine and his BS in Biochemistry from the University of Minnesota, Twin Cities. His honors include the Young Investigator Award from the Society for Immunotherapy of Cancer (SITC) and a K22 Career Transition Award from the National Cancer Institute.

Learn more on his [Johns Hopkins faculty profile](https://profiles.hopkinsmedicine.org/provider/dimitri-sidiropoulos/2777687).
